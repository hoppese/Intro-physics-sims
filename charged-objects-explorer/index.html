What this practices

The standard intro-E&M repertoire in one sandbox: charging by friction (rub a cloth on a rod — the triboelectric series decides who ends up + and who ends up −), by contact (touch a charged object to a neutral one and they share), and by induction (bring a charge near a grounded conductor, disconnect the ground while it's still near, then remove the charge — the conductor keeps a net charge opposite the inducer, without ever touching it).

Objects
Sphere — a conductor. Charge roams freely on it: contact-shares, polarizes near other charges, and its net charge can be drained or set by grounding.
Rod — an insulator with a material (glass, plastic/rubber, …). Doesn't share charge by touching; only rubbing changes it.
Cloth — the rubbing partner, also has a material. Rubbing a rod and a cloth together charges both, oppositely.
Pith ball — a light conductor on a thread. Swings toward anything it's attracted to — including neutral objects it can polarize.
Electroscope — the detector. Its leaf deflects by , regardless of sign; watch it to see how much (and, by which way it kicks, which sign of) charge is present.
Charge distribution

Toggle it on to see the actual (signed) surface charge — patches on a conductor, a faint tint on an insulator. It's the "answer key" for what you predicted from the deflection or the attraction alone.

Worked example ×

Charging a sphere by induction

1 · Add a sphere and ground it (drag the ground token onto it). With nothing nearby, it sits at zero net charge.

2 · Charge a rod (rub a cloth on it) and drag it close to the sphere without touching. Watch the distribution toggle: the near side of the sphere shows charge opposite the rod, the far side shows charge draining away through the ground wire.

3 · Drag the ground token off the sphere — it's now an isolated conductor, still near the rod, holding a net charge opposite the rod's sign.

4 · Pull the rod far away. The sphere's charge — still opposite the rod's original sign — spreads out uniformly. No contact ever happened.
