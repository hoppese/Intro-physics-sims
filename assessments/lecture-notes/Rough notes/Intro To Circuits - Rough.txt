Battery & Resistor Circuits

Start with definitions:

- Current: Charge passing through an elelment a point per second, 1 A = 1 C/s
- Change in Voltage: Potential energy per charge over an element
- Series - elements connected "back-to-back"
- Parallel - elements connected "side-by-side"

Rules:

- Elements in series have same current passing through them
- Elements in parallel have same voltage change over them
- Current is conserved at junctions
- Change in Voltage over the whole loop sums to zero
