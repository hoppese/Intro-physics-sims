Electric potential 

Does height give you potential energy?
	-> You still need 2 things:
		1) The mass of the object
		2) Height relative to where?

Electric potential is like height
	-> You can get EPE if you provide 2 things:
		1) The amount of charge
		2) Potential relative to where?

Electric potential is PE per charge => V = U/q, measured in Volts = J/C
Height tells you PE per mass

Parallel plate capacitor
	-> Each plate gives \eta/2 \eps_0
	-> Total field is \eta/\eps_0
	-> Uniform E field away from edges
	-> Does the potential stay constant when the E-field is constant?
		- Does GPE stay constant when force of gravity is constant? No!
