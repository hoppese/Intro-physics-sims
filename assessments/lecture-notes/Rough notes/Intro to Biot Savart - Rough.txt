Intro to single charge Biot-Savart law

Start with Coulomb's law
	- Find E field from one +2 micro coulomb charge at 3 locations.
		- Charge at (-5,0,0) cm. Locations (-9,3,0), (-5,3,0), (-1,3,0).
		- Compute the separation vectors first.
		- Compute unit separation vectors.

Then introduce Biot-Savart
	- Find B field from same charge moving in +x direction at +12 m/s
		- Reuse unit separation vectors
	- Compare magnitudes and directions of E & B fields.
	- Then, 3 charges moving in a line, computing the B field at one location.
		- Have them moving in the +y direction, not on the y axis
		- Field point to the left of the charges
		- Compute the separation vectors and their unit vectors explicitly
