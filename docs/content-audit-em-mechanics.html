Fixed this pass

diffraction-pattern — Info blurb described the wrong thing. "What this shows" read: "Light passing a single slit spreads into a diffraction pattern … (a·sinθ = mλ)." But a·sinθ = mλ is the single-slit minima condition, not "the pattern," and the sim actually defaults to Double mode with Single / Double / Grating tabs — its primary Info formula and its worked example are the multi-slit d·sinθ = mλ maxima. Rewritten to cover all three modes without presenting the minima formula as the pattern.
faraday-flux-explorer — "flip" contradicted its own arithmetic. Worked-example step 4 was titled "Flip it in 0.25 s" but computed ΔΦ = 0.010 Wb — a 90° quarter-turn from face-on to edge-on, not a 180° flip (which is ΔΦ = 0.020). Retitled "Turn it edge-on in 0.25 s" to match the number. (0.010/0.25 = 0.04 V is right for the edge-on case.)
