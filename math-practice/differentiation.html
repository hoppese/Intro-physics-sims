What this practices

Taking derivatives — the slope of the tangent line, and the rate-of-change machinery behind velocity, acceleration and every "how fast is X changing" question in physics. The variable is not always x and the function is not always f; read each problem.

Levels
1 — power rule: differentiate a cubic, give the coefficients of the derivative.
2 — product rule: slope at a point.
3 — chain rule: slope at a point.
4 — sine & cosine: slope at an angle.
5 — exponential & natural log: slope at a point.
6 — minima & maxima: find where the derivative is zero and classify.
