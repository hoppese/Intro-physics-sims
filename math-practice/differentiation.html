What this practices

Finding a derivative — the slope of the tangent line, and the rate-of-change machinery behind velocity, acceleration, and every "how fast is X changing" question in physics. Level 1 asks for the derivative polynomial's coefficients directly; levels 2–4 ask for the numeric slope at a given point, using the product, chain, and trig/exponential rules.

How to use

Read off f(x), apply the relevant rule, and enter your answer. Checking reveals the tangent line on the graph (levels 2–4) so you can see the slope you just computed.
