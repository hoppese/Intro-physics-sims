What this practices

Reading sine, cosine, and tangent straight off the unit circle — the skill that underlies every projection, component, and phase calculation in physics (resolving a force into x/y, phase of an oscillator, AC voltage at time t). Levels move from degrees with sin/cos only, to adding tangent, to radians, to working backward from a trig value to the angle.

How to use

The terminal ray and its projections onto the axes are drawn for you — cosine is the horizontal projection, sine the vertical one. All angles are "nice" (multiples of 15°) so every answer is an exact value like ½ or √2/2 ≈ 0.707; enter decimals.
