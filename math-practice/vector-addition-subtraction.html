What this practices

Every vector skill physics leans on: resolving into components, going back to magnitude & direction, adding and subtracting, and — the one that trips people up — resolving a vector onto a set of tilted axes (an inclined plane). Angle names and vector names change between problems on purpose; don't memorise "θ" and "x".

Levels
1 — magnitude & angle → components.
2 — components → magnitude & direction.
3 — add / subtract two vectors (2D components).
4 — add / subtract in 3D (components).
5 — add / subtract when one vector is polar; answer as magnitude & direction.
6 — resolve a vector onto axes rotated by φ.
7 — split a vector into components parallel and perpendicular to an inclined plane.
