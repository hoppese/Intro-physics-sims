What this practices

Adding and subtracting vectors by components — the skill under almost every physics problem involving forces, velocities, or fields. Levels progress from plain component addition to converting magnitude/angle (polar) form into components, and finally reporting the resultant itself as magnitude & direction.

How to use

Read the given vectors, type your answer, and hit Check. Tip-to-tail construction is drawn on the canvas once you check, so you can see geometrically why the answer works. Miss two in a row and the level drops back down — no penalty, just fewer moving parts while you rebuild the skill.
