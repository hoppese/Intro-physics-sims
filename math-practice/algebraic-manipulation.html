What this practices

Rearranging equations — the single most-used skill in physics problem-solving, since every formula eventually has to be solved for whatever you're actually after. The unknown is not always x: read the "Solve for" label each time.

Levels
1 — one- and two-step linear equations, solve for a number.
2 — variable on both sides, and distributing, solve for a number.
3 — rearrange a formula (×, ÷, +, −) for a target variable — pick the correct form.
4 — rearrange with squares and square roots.
5 — rearrange with trig functions, logs, or exponentials.
6 — fractional and other powers, multi-step.
Adaptive

Three correct in a row moves you up a level; two wrong in a row moves you down. Opening "Show worked solution" before you answer means that problem is not scored.
