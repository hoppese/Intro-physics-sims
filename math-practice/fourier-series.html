The dashed grey curve is the target; the solid curve is the N-term partial sum; the strip underneath is the residual f − SN (watch the spikes at jumps — that's Gibbs).

Practice levels
1 — period T, fundamental ω₀ = 2π/T, and whether even harmonics appear.
2 — symmetry: which coefficients vanish (even → bn=0, odd → an=0, half-wave → even harmonics gone).
3 — the constant term a₀ (= twice the average).
4 — an or bn for a specific n of a standard waveform.
5 — evaluate an N-term partial sum, or the Gibbs overshoot.
