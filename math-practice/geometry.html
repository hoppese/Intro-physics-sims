What this practices

The geometry that shows up constantly in physics setup work: right-triangle sides (resolving vectors, ramps), areas and circumferences (pressure = force/area, flux through a loop), similar-triangle ratios (optics, shadows, lens diagrams), and volumes (density, buoyancy). Levels move from the Pythagorean theorem to 2D area/circumference, to similar triangles, to 3D volumes.

How to use

Read the labeled figure and compute the requested quantity. Figures are drawn roughly to scale (not exact) so you can sanity-check whether your answer's size makes sense.
