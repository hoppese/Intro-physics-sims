What this practices

Antiderivatives and definite integrals — the running-total counterpart to differentiation, behind every "area under the curve" quantity in physics (displacement from velocity, work from force, impulse from a force-time graph). Level 1 asks for the antiderivative's coefficients; levels 2–4 ask for the numeric value of a definite integral, using polynomial, chain-rule (u-substitution), and trig/exponential antiderivatives.

How to use

Find the antiderivative, then (for levels 2–4) evaluate it at the two bounds and subtract. Checking shades the actual area on the graph so you can sanity-check sign and rough size before trusting the number.
